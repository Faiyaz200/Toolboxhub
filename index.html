About ToolBoxHub

ToolBoxHub is a simple free online toolbox built to make everyday file, text, security and developer tasks easier from one place.

Many tools process your files directly in your browser, so you can work quickly without installing a desktop app. Some features may use third-party web services or libraries when required.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

ToolBoxHub is designed with privacy in mind. Where a tool supports browser-side processing, your selected files are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to ToolBoxHub's own server.

Some features may contact third-party services, such as external libraries or QR-generation services, to provide functionality. Those services may receive the information required for the request. Do not use ToolBoxHub for highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable with the relevant service's policies.

ToolBoxHub may use basic analytics or advertising services in the future. If that happens, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices and choices.
